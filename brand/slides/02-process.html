Our Process

Seven steps, one team.

1
Initial Design Consultation

We learn what you're envisioning, how you want the space to feel, and what priorities matter most.

2
Design Preview

In 5–7 days we return with 2D and 3D renderings plus a detailed, itemized estimate.

3
Deposit and HOA Submission

HOA plans are typically submitted within 3–4 days of deposit so approval can begin quickly.

4
Commencement

Construction generally starts 1–2 weeks after approval as materials and logistics are finalized.
