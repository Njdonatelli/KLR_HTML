## Purpose
Turn an approved design + site data into a complete, **margin-protected**, itemized estimate structured for client comparison and mapped to a milestone payment schedule. Delivers on the flyer promise of "detailed, itemized estimates and an organized payment structure."

## Scope / Trigger
Fires when DES-004 renderings are internally approved and DES-001 site data is complete. Covers quantity takeoff, pricing, sub scopes, fees, margin application, and estimate structuring. Ends when a margin-checked estimate is ready for DES-006 (proposal). Does **not** cover proposal presentation (DES-006) or contract (CON-001).

## Roles (RACI-lite)
- **Responsible:** Design Lead / Estimator
- **Accountable:** Design Lead
- **Consulted:** Production Lead (constructability, labor hours); subs (specialty quotes)
- **Informed:** Operations Manager / Principal (margin approval gate)

## Systems & Inputs
- **Contractor Foreman** — estimate build + payment schedule.
- **PRC-001 Approved Supplier List** — SiteOne (hardscape/irrigation), nurseries (plant material).
- Approved design (DES-004), measured site + drainage data (DES-001), HOA/permit requirements (CON-002/CON-003).

## Procedure
1. **Input gate** — confirm approved design, complete site + drainage data, and HOA/permit requirements are in hand. **Do not estimate on incomplete data** — bounce to DES-001/DES-004 if gaps exist. → *Output: verified input set.*
2. **Quantity takeoff** by category from design + measurements: hardscape SF + base, paver/unit counts, drainage components, pool/spa allowances, planting counts, turf SF, irrigation zones, structures. → *Output: takeoff sheet.*
3. **Price materials** against **PRC-001** approved suppliers; flag volatile-price and long-lead items. **No guessed pricing.** → *Output: priced materials.*
4. **Labor + equipment** estimate by phase (excavation, base, install, planting), consulting Production Lead on hours. Include rentals. → *Output: labor/equipment lines.*
5. **Subcontractor scopes** — solicit quotes (pool, electrical, gas, specialty) or apply time-boxed allowances where quotes aren't yet available. → *Output: sub line items (quoted or allowance-flagged).*
6. **Fees** — permit (CON-003), HOA, disposal/dump, inspections. → *Output: fee lines.*
7. **Margin + contingency** — apply overhead and **target gross margin ≥ [__%]**; add a contingency/allowance line sized to subgrade/drainage uncertainty. → *Output: internal cost sheet with margin check.*
8. **Structure client-facing estimate** — itemize by phase/category with **alternates/options** so the client can compare scopes (flyer intent); map totals to a **milestone payment schedule** (feeds FIN-001). → *Output: itemized estimate + payment schedule.*
9. **Approval gate** — Ops Manager/Principal sign-off if total > **[$ threshold]** or GM < target. Log approval. → *Output: approved estimate.*
10. **Release** to DES-006 for proposal assembly.

## Acceptance Criteria / QC
**No estimate leaves without a margin check.** Concretely:
- Every design element maps to at least one estimate line (no unpriced scope).
- All materials priced against PRC-001 suppliers — zero guessed prices.
- Target GM met, or exception explicitly approved and logged.
- Contingency line present.
- Client-facing estimate itemized with alternates; payment schedule maps to defined milestones.
- Internal approval recorded where thresholds trigger it.

## Exceptions & Escalation
- **GM below target** → Principal approval required to proceed.
- **High-uncertainty scope** → convert to a clearly labeled allowance; note assumption to client via DES-006.
- **Sub quote unavailable in time** → time-boxed allowance, flagged as provisional.
- **Material price spike / long lead** → note validity window on the estimate; re-price if signed past window.

## Outputs
Internal margin-checked cost sheet + client-facing itemized estimate + milestone payment schedule → feeds **DES-006** (proposal). On win: **CON-001** (contract) and **FIN-001** (billing); actuals reconcile against **FIN-003** (job costing).
