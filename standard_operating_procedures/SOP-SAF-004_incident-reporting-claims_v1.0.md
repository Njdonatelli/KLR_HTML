## Purpose
Ensure every jobsite incident — injury, property damage, or near-miss — is responded to, documented, and reported to insurance and authorities correctly and **within statutory deadlines** — protecting people, limiting liability, and preserving coverage/claim rights.

## Scope / Trigger
Fires on any incident: worker injury, third-party/property damage, vehicle incident, or near-miss. Covers immediate response through claim closure and corrective action.

## Roles (RACI-lite)
- **Responsible:** On-site crew lead (immediate) → Operations Manager (reporting/claim)
- **Accountable:** Operations Manager
- **Consulted:** Insurance broker, counsel (serious incidents), Principal
- **Informed:** Affected worker, Admin/Finance

## Systems & Inputs
- **Dropbox** — incident records + claim docs (TEC-002).
- Incident form; insurance policies (SAF-005); emergency contacts.

## Procedure
1. **Immediate response** — secure the scene, render/summon medical aid, make the area safe. Life-safety first. → *Output: scene safe.*
2. **Internal report same-day** to Operations Manager / Principal. → *Output: internal report.*
3. **Document** — incident form: who/what/when/where, witnesses, photos, conditions. → *Output: incident record.*
4. **Notify within deadlines** — insurer per policy; **workers' comp per CA employer timeline**; **Cal/OSHA for serious injury/fatality within the required window [verify current deadlines]**. → *Output: notifications logged.*
5. **Manage the claim** — single point of contact; preserve evidence; cooperate with insurer/investigators. → *Output: claim managed.*
6. **Corrective action** — root-cause; update SAF-001/002 if systemic. → *Output: corrective action logged.*

## Acceptance Criteria / QC
**Every incident is documented same-day and reported within statutory/policy deadlines — no exceptions.** Concretely:
- Scene secured; medical rendered where needed.
- Incident documented same-day (form + photos + witnesses).
- Insurer + required authorities notified within deadlines.
- Claim managed to closure with evidence preserved.
- Corrective action logged; source SOPs updated if systemic.

## Exceptions & Escalation
- **Serious injury / fatality** → immediate Principal + counsel + Cal/OSHA per mandatory reporting window (verify); do not disturb scene beyond making safe.
- **Disputed liability / third-party claim** → counsel before statements.

## Outputs
Incident record, notifications, claim file, corrective action → Dropbox. Feeds **SAF-001/002** (prevention) and interacts with **SAF-005** (coverage) / **HR-004** (worker status).
