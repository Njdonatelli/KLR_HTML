## Purpose
Assemble the renders (DES-004), itemized estimate (DES-005), and payment schedule (FIN-001) into one client-facing proposal that presents the design with confidence, lets the client compare options, and drives a decision — the conversion artifact that, on acceptance, triggers CON-001.

## Scope / Trigger
Fires when DES-004 renders are approved and DES-005 estimate is margin-checked. Covers proposal assembly, internal review, delivery/presentation, and disposition. Ends by triggering CON-001 (accept) or a coded close/nurture (decline/stall).

## Roles (RACI-lite)
- **Responsible:** Design Lead
- **Accountable:** Design Lead
- **Consulted:** Operations Manager (terms/margin), Sales Lead (close)
- **Informed:** Client, Principal (non-standard deals)

## Systems & Inputs
- **Contractor Foreman** — proposal build.
- Approved renders (DES-004), margin-checked estimate (DES-005), payment schedule (FIN-001).

## Procedure
1. **Input gate** — confirm approved renders, margin-checked estimate, and payment schedule are all present and internally approved. → *Output: verified inputs.*
2. **Assemble proposal** — brand cover, design renders, itemized scope **with alternates/options**, payment schedule, construction timeline, the 7-step process, and terms summary. → *Output: proposal draft.*
3. **Internal review** — pricing/margin, scope accuracy vs. renders, no un-permittable/undrainable promises. → *Output: reviewed proposal.*
4. **Deliver + present** (M365 / meeting); walk the client through options. → *Output: delivered proposal.*
5. **Handle Q&A + options** — minor changes within scope; material changes loop back to DES-004/DES-005. → *Output: client feedback.*
6. **Disposition** — **accept → trigger CON-001**; stall → nurture/follow-up cadence; decline → reason-coded close (feeds SAL-004). → *Output: disposition.*

## Acceptance Criteria / QC
**No proposal goes out missing renders, itemized pricing, or the payment schedule — the client can compare and decide from one document.** Concretely:
- Proposal contains renders + itemized estimate (with alternates) + payment schedule + timeline + process.
- Internally reviewed for margin and scope-vs-render accuracy.
- Delivered/presented to the client.
- Outcome dispositioned (accept/stall/decline) with reason.

## Exceptions & Escalation
- **Material scope change requested** → loop to DES-004/DES-005; re-issue, don't hand-edit.
- **Non-standard terms/discount** → Principal.
- **Stalled past [14 days]** → Sales Lead nurture; revisit pricing validity window (DES-005).

## Outputs
Client-facing proposal → on acceptance triggers **CON-001** (contract/deposit); decline data → **SAL-004**.
