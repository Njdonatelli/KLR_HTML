## Purpose
Correctly classify every worker — employee (W2) vs. independent contractor (1099) — under CA law **before engagement**, avoiding misclassification liability (back wages, taxes, penalties).

## Scope / Trigger
Fires before engaging any worker (HR-002/003) or subcontractor (PRO-004), plus periodic re-review on role change. Covers the classification determination and its documentation.

## Roles (RACI-lite)
- **Responsible:** Principal (or Ops Manager)
- **Accountable:** Principal
- **Consulted:** Employment counsel, CPA
- **Informed:** Admin/Finance (payroll vs. AP), Production Lead (sub engagement)

## Systems & Inputs
- **Dropbox** — classification records (`02_Areas/HR/`, TEC-002).
- CA ABC test (AB5) criteria; sub license (SAF-005) + COI + written agreement.

## Classification logic (verify with counsel)
**CA ABC test — worker is an employee unless ALL of:**
- **A** — free from control/direction in performing the work;
- **B** — work is **outside KLR's usual course of business**;
- **C** — engaged in an independently established trade/business of the same type.

> Practical implication: **core construction labor almost always fails prong B → W2 employee.** Licensed subcontractors operating as separate businesses may qualify differently (see CA construction-subcontractor provisions) — verify per engagement.

## Procedure
1. **Run the ABC test** before engaging any worker; determine W2 vs. 1099. → *Output: classification determination.*
2. **Core construction labor → W2** (default); document the rationale; route to HR-003 onboarding. → *Output: W2 record.*
3. **Licensed subcontractor path** — verify license (SAF-005), COI, and a written sub agreement; classify 1099 **only** if independent-business criteria are met. → *Output: sub classification.*
4. **Document determination + basis**; retain. → *Output: classification record.*
5. **Re-review** on role change; annual audit of active classifications. → *Output: audit record.*
6. **Escalate ambiguous cases** to counsel/CPA **before** engagement. → *Output: cleared determination.*

## Acceptance Criteria / QC
**No worker is engaged before classification is determined and documented; core construction labor defaults to W2.** Concretely:
- ABC-test determination made pre-engagement with documented rationale.
- Core labor classified W2.
- 1099 used only with verified independent-business criteria + license + COI + written agreement.
- Determinations retained; annual audit performed.

## Exceptions & Escalation
- **Ambiguous or borderline case** → counsel/CPA before engaging; do not default to 1099 for convenience.
- **Existing misclassification discovered** → Principal + counsel for remediation.

## Outputs
Documented classification determinations → Dropbox. Gates **HR-003** (onboarding path) and **PRO-004** (sub engagement); informs **FIN-004** (payroll vs. AP) and ties to **SAF-005** (sub credentials).
