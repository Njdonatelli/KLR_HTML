## Purpose
Guarantee positive drainage away from structures and hardscape on every project. This is the flyer's stated differentiator ("protect foundations, paving, and planting from water damage and settling") and the **single highest callback/warranty risk** — standardizing it is why FLD-002 is P1 despite being field craft.

## Scope / Trigger
Fires at the grading/drainage phase (after site prep FLD-001, before base/hardscape FLD-003). Covers subgrade grading, surface slope, and drainage-system installation. Governed by the design drainage strategy from DES-001/DES-004. Does **not** cover the paver assembly itself (FLD-003).

## Roles (RACI-lite)
- **Responsible:** Production Lead / crew lead
- **Accountable:** Production Lead
- **Consulted:** Design Lead (design intent), civil engineer (where plans are engineered)
- **Informed:** Operations Manager (inspection/gate status)

## Systems & Inputs
- Drainage notes + grades (DES-001), plans (DES-004), drainage components from takeoff (DES-005 / PRO-003).
- Laser level / transit, compaction equipment.

## Specifications (verify before Effective)
| Item | Target | Governing ref (verify) |
|------|--------|------------------------|
| Slope away from foundation | **≥ 6" fall over first 10 ft (~5%)** | IRC grading @ foundation |
| Finished hardscape surface slope | **1%–2% (≈ ⅛"–¼" per ft)** min positive | ICPI / manufacturer |
| Subgrade compaction | **[≥ 95% Proctor]**, uniform, no soft spots | Geotech / manufacturer |
| Drainage discharge | To daylight / approved point; **never onto neighboring property** | Local code / civil |
| Downspout tie-ins | Captured + routed to drainage system | Plan-specific |

## Procedure
1. **Verify design + code** — confirm the drainage strategy (DES-004) and required slopes against local code / engineered plan before moving dirt. → *Output: confirmed grade plan.*
2. **Rough grade subgrade** to design contours; establish continuous positive fall away from all structures per spec. → *Output: rough-graded subgrade.*
3. **Compact subgrade** to spec; correct soft spots (over-excavate + replace/compact). → *Output: compacted subgrade.*
4. **Install drainage system** per plan — French drains, channel/trench drains, catch basins, downspout tie-ins; maintain pipe fall to daylight/approved discharge. → *Output: installed drainage.*
5. **Verify discharge** routes to an approved point and not onto adjacent property. → *Output: verified discharge path.*
6. **Confirm finish grade + slope** with laser/transit across the whole surface before base goes in (FLD-003). → *Output: grade-verified surface + photo record.*
7. **Water test** where feasible (hose/observed rain) on drainage-critical areas; confirm no ponding, correct flow. → *Output: drainage test record.*

## Acceptance Criteria / QC
**No base or hardscape is placed until grade and drainage are verified and documented.** Concretely:
- Positive slope away from structures meets spec across the entire area (laser-verified).
- Subgrade compaction meets spec; no soft spots.
- Drainage system installed to plan with continuous fall to an approved discharge — not onto neighboring property.
- Downspouts captured.
- Grade + drainage photographed; water test logged where feasible.
- Any required inspection passed (PRO-002 hold point).

## Exceptions & Escalation
- **Field conditions conflict with design slope** (e.g., insufficient fall available) → stop; Design Lead + engineer for a revised drainage solution (may trigger CON-004).
- **Unsuitable/expansive soils** discovered → geotech consult; document as unforeseen condition (CON-004).
- **Discharge point dispute / no legal outlet** → Operations Manager + Principal before proceeding.

## Outputs
Verified grade + installed/tested drainage + photo and test records → Dropbox project folder. Releases **FLD-003** (base/hardscape); records feed **CX-001** sign-off and **CX-002** warranty baseline.
