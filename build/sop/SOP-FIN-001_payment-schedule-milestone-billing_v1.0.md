## Purpose
Define a contract-embedded, **CA-compliant** milestone payment schedule (schedule of values) that maps to production phases and drives invoicing (FIN-002). Delivers the flyer's "organized payment structure" while keeping each draw within legal limits.

## Scope / Trigger
Fires during DES-005/DES-006 (schedule built into the proposal) and locks at CON-001 (embedded in the executed contract). Adjusted on CON-004. Releases billing triggers to FIN-002 through the build. Covers structure and release triggers — not invoice issuance (FIN-002).

## Roles (RACI-lite)
- **Responsible:** Admin / Finance
- **Accountable:** Operations Manager
- **Consulted:** Design Lead (SOV allocation), Principal (non-standard structures), counsel (compliance)
- **Informed:** Production Lead (milestone triggers), Client (schedule at signing)

## Compliance guardrails (verify with counsel)
| Rule | Constraint |
|------|-----------|
| Down payment | **≤ lesser of $1,000 or 10%** of contract price (CON-001) |
| Progress payments | **Each draw ≤ value of work performed + materials delivered** to date — no billing ahead of work |
| Special-order materials | May be billable on delivery **[verify permitted structure]** |
| Final payment | Due on completion / acceptance (CX-001) |

## Procedure
1. **Build the schedule of values** — allocate the contract sum (from DES-005) across phases/milestones. → *Output: SOV.*
2. **Set the deposit** within the statutory cap (CON-001). → *Output: compliant deposit line.*
3. **Structure draws** so each milestone bills **no more than the value of work performed** at that point; tie each to a **verifiable completion trigger** (phase complete per PRO-002 + PRO-005, inspection pass). → *Output: triggered draw schedule.*
4. **Embed in contract** (CON-001) before signature; present to client. → *Output: contracted payment schedule.*
5. **Adjust on change** — reflect CON-004 change orders in the SOV + contract sum. → *Output: updated schedule.*
6. **Release milestones** to FIN-002 for invoicing only when the completion trigger is verified (PRO-005 / PRO-006). → *Output: billing releases.*

## Acceptance Criteria / QC
**No draw is billed before its work is verifiably complete, and no draw exceeds the value of work performed.** Concretely:
- Payment schedule embedded in the contract before signature.
- Deposit within statutory cap.
- Each milestone tied to a verifiable completion trigger.
- CO adjustments reflected in SOV + contract sum.
- No front-loaded/ahead-of-work billing.

## Exceptions & Escalation
- **Client requests a front-loaded schedule** → not permitted; explain the statutory limit, keep compliant.
- **Large special-order material cost** → structure a compliant materials-on-delivery draw (verify with counsel).
- **Non-standard structure requested** → Principal + counsel.

## Outputs
Schedule of values / payment schedule → contract (CON-001); verified billing triggers → FIN-002; cost baseline → FIN-003.
