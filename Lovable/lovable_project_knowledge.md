Project overview
This is a marketing website and design system for KLR Build LLC, a family-owned outdoor living design-build company based in Oceanside, CA. They design and build patios, hardscape, pools, and more. 
Tagline: "Designed with intent. Built to endure."

Target audience
Homeowners in the Oceanside/San Diego area looking for high-quality outdoor living spaces built by a reliable, local, family-owned team.

Brand voice & copy
- Tone: Direct, reassuring, and a little folksy. Short and concrete sentences. 
- Perspective: Speak about "you" (the client) and "we" (the team). Emphasize being "family-owned".
- Style: No emoji, no slang, no hyperbole. Confidence comes from specificity.
- Casing: Section headers and callouts (eyebrows/labels/taglines) should be ALL CAPS. Body paragraphs are normal sentence case. 

Design guidelines
- Vibe: Warm, grounded, craftsman. Earthy, muted palette, not bright.
- Colors: Navy (#20404e) is the primary accent. Olive (#5f6043) and bronze (#7e6e52) are secondary accents. Tan (#b4a593) is a warm highlight. Charcoal (#181e23) for text.
- Typography: Archivo (700-900) for display/heading. Work Sans (400-600) for body. Barlow Semi Condensed (500-700, uppercase, wide tracking) for labels/eyebrows.
- Backgrounds: Flat color fields (navy or off-white). No gradients, textures, or patterns.
- Imagery: Use real job-site/finished-space photography. No generic stock.
- Borders & shadows: 1px hairline borders (warm stone gray). Soft, low-elevation shadows (never heavy/dark).
- Corner radii: 4px (inputs/buttons), 8px (small cards), 14px (larger content cards), pill (badges only).
- Layout: Centered container, max width 1200px.
- Iconography: No custom icons. Use Lucide for functional UI icons (thin stroke). No emoji.

Animation & interactions
- Restrained, functional motion only. 120-180ms ease transitions on hover/focus.
- No bounce, no scale/shrink effects. Keep interactions calm and solid.
- Hover states: Buttons darken (navy -> lighter navy) or gain a light tint fill.

Architecture & layout rules
- Structure: Lean on short numbered/stepped lists (e.g., 7-step process) and small stat call-outs rather than long paragraphs.
- Logos on dark: Do not place the full-color mark directly on navy or charcoal backgrounds; place it on a light chip/panel.
